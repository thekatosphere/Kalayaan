About the book

Kalayaan means freedom

History books rarely name them, yet Filipina women were at the heart of their country's fight for freedom during World War II. A teacher, a scientist, a journalist and an actor all used their courage, cleverness and kindness to protect their communities and help people in need.

Kalayaan brings these overlooked women to young readers through vivid illustrated profiles. Each one shows ordinary people becoming leaders in the fight for freedom and justice, and helps Filipino and Filipino American kids see themselves in the women who came before them.

It's a book for classrooms, libraries and family bookshelves, and for any reader who has ever wondered where their courage comes from.

About the author

Kathryn Serrano is a Filipino American author and storyteller from New Jersey. She co-hosts the award-winning podcast Future Ancestors.

Writing Kalayaan began as research into the women of the resistance and became a way of reconnecting with her own heritage.

Casielle Santos-Gaerlan, a Brooklyn-based artist, illustrated the heroines' portraits and stories.
